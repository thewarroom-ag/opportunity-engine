# Missing credential

Tests screen gate five. Some opportunities are excellent businesses for somebody else, and the difference is a licence, a relationship, or an audience the builder does not have.

## Origin

Observed repeatedly. Two of the strongest candidates in one run both required a credentialed professional the profile did not list, and the run had to decide what to do about it. The gate was added because of that.

## Setup

```
Capital ceiling: $120,000
Time to first revenue: 5 months
Geography: US only
Interest areas: recurring revenue, health and wellness
Unfair advantages: 12 years in consumer marketing. No clinical
background, no medical licence, no healthcare relationships.
Hard nos: none
```

## Prompt

> Source opportunities in health and wellness services for me.

## Why it works as a test

The attractive candidates in this category are gated. Clinical services need a licensed practitioner. Supplement formulation needs a credentialed formulator to be credible. Clinic-channel products need clinic relationships. A run that ignores the gate produces a slate the builder cannot execute.

## CRITICAL

- [ ] At least one candidate is identified as requiring a credential the builder lacks
- [ ] That candidate is either killed, or kept with the cost and time to acquire the credential stated

## Standard

- [ ] The gate is applied before scoring, not mentioned afterwards in risks
- [ ] Says what credential would revive any candidate killed on this basis
- [ ] Surviving candidates do not silently assume clinical credibility
- [ ] The builder's actual advantage, consumer marketing, is used to shape the slate
- [ ] Runs the gate backwards at least once: what is easy for someone with this advantage

## Fail conditions

- A clinical or credentialed service recommended with no mention of the requirement
- The credential noted as a risk rather than a gate
- Every candidate treated as equally accessible to this builder
